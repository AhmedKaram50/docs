The Identity module is the authentication, authorization, and tenant-management layer of mab-api. It owns:

- **Accounts** — humans with email + password
- **Shops** — tenants (one per Shopify store)
- **Memberships** — join between accounts and shops
- **Invites** — shop-scoped, email-targeted, one-time
- **Refresh tokens** — opaque, hashed, rotated
- **Subscriptions** — per-shop trial/billing state

## Aggregates

```
Account ─── Membership ─── Shop ─── Subscription
                              │
                              ├── Invite (one-time, per email + shop)
                              │
                              └── RefreshToken (account-scoped, but issued via shop login)
```

- **`Account`** — global. Email unique across the system. Password hashed with argon2.
- **`Shop`** — tenant. Identified by `shopifyDomain`. Optionally holds an encrypted Shopify access token (AES-GCM).
- **`Membership`** — `(account_id, shop_id)` unique. Created on register, invite-accept, or set-password.
- **`Invite`** — `(email, shop_id)` with one-time token (sha256-hashed in DB).
- **`Subscription`** — one per shop. Tiers: `starter | growth | pro`. Status: `trialing | active | canceled | expired`.
- **`RefreshToken`** — `account_id` + `token_hash` + lifetimes. Rotated on every refresh.
